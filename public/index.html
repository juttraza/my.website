Our story

Cooking that starts at the farm gate.

Ember & Oak began as a single hearth and a handful of tables. Today we still cook everything over wood, buy from growers we know by name, and keep the menu short so every plate gets full attention.

  • SeasonalMenu rewritten every few weeks, following what is ripe.
  • LocalProduce, dairy and meat from farms within 100 km.
  • Open fireOak and fruitwood only. Slow, honest, full of flavour.
